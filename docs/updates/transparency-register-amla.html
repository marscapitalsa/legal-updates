Transparency register and revised AMLA begin on 1 October 2026

The new federal register changes both the IAM’s position as a Swiss legal entity and the information available to it as an AMLA-supervised financial intermediary.

Key facts

  • LETA, the revised AMLA and implementing ordinances enter into force on 1 October 2026; this date starts applicable registration transition periods.
  • The register records individuals who control Swiss entities and certain foreign entities linked to Switzerland.
  • Access is limited to specified authorities and AMLA-subject persons; it is not a public company register.

Why it matters

  • The firm should determine its own reporting duty, beneficial owners and submission responsibility.
  • The register may support KYC, but it should not be treated as a substitute for risk-based beneficial-owner verification.
  • Differences between client-provided information and register data need an escalation and documentation route.
Suggested follow-up: appoint an owner, prepare corporate beneficial-owner data, establish access rights, and update onboarding procedures for register checks and discrepancy handling.
