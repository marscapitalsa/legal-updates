EU / UK / US · Foreign-regime updates

July Russia sanctions: new EU and UK measures and targeted US licences

What changed: the EU adopted a 21st sanctions package on 23 July, the UK announced Russia-related cyber designations on 13 July, and OFAC issued targeted Russia-related general-licence updates on 8 and 24 July. These publications do not by themselves amend Swiss sanctions law. [S1] [S2] [S3] [S4]

Evidence
No general evidence requirement for a Swiss IAM arises from these foreign measures.

July developments

  • EU, 23 July: the 21st package added 48 individuals and 170 entities and targeted Russian energy, financial services and crypto, among other sectors. [S1]
  • UK, 13 July: the UK announced coordinated sanctions against 24 persons and entities associated with Russian state-linked cyber activity and cybercrime. [S2]
  • US, 8 July: OFAC issued Russia-related General License 13R. [S3]
  • US, 24 July: OFAC issued General License 131H concerning specified transactions involving Lukoil International GmbH and updated FAQs 1224 and 1225. [S4]
  • US, 20 and 27 July: the OFAC notices concern administrative list maintenance rather than a new Russian industrial-entity designation. [S5] [S6]

Swiss-law limitation

These foreign measures do not themselves amend Swiss sanctions law. Swiss implementation requires a corresponding SECO or Federal Council measure. The current Swiss position should therefore be checked before taking a legal or operational decision.
