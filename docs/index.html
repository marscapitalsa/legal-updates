Potentially relevant
FINMA · 03 Jun

FINMA sharpens expectations for products used in individual portfolio management

What changed: FINMA set out concrete expectations after cases involving unsuitable, illiquid, concentrated and insufficiently reviewed products. Relevant if: the IAM launches or uses an AMC or another higher-risk product; approval, enhanced due diligence, liquidity, conflicts and monitoring must then be documented before use.

FINMA · 04 Jun

AML risk analysis must function as a real management control

What changed: FINMA found continuing gaps between AML risk analyses, approved tolerance, measurable limits and control effectiveness. What the IAM should do: update and approve the analysis by 31 August 2026.

Federal Council · 12 Jun

Transparency register and revised AMLA start on 1 October 2026

What changed: the commencement date for the beneficial-owner register and revised AMLA framework is 1 October 2026. What the IAM should do: prepare entity reporting and the separate KYC/discrepancy workflow by 30 September 2026.

SAM / SECO · Jul

SECO search results can no longer be printed or saved as before

What changed: the former print/PDF output is unavailable. What the IAM should do: by 7 August 2026, amend procedures so each search retains a dated screenshot or equivalent record of the query, result, operator and reviewer.
