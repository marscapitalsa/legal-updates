COMCO consultation · Medium relevance

Competition law applies to labour-market coordination

COMCO’s draft note follows findings that more than 200 companies exchanged detailed wage and employment information over years outside social-partnership arrangements.

Key facts

  • The consultation runs until 30 September 2026; a consolidated note is expected by year-end.
  • The draft identifies labour-market agreements that may breach Swiss cartel law.
  • The issue can include sensitive wage exchanges and other coordination between employers.

Why it matters

  • Financial-sector peer groups and compensation surveys can create risks if current, granular or identifiable data are exchanged.
  • No-poach or non-solicitation arrangements between employers require legal review.
  • HR, management and industry-association representatives need the same competition-law awareness as commercial teams.
Suggested follow-up: identify wage benchmarking, peer-group exchanges and inter-firm hiring restrictions; preserve appropriate aggregation and independence and refer questionable arrangements for competition-law review.
