29 Jun 2026FINMA · EnforcementAction may be required

FINMA concluded enforcement proceedings involving a fund manager and a portfolio-manager applicant. The cases concerned conflicts of interest and serious FinSA suitability and appropriateness failures, including exposure to illiquid products.

Open primary source ↗		Relevant because FINMA is testing whether firms can evidence product selection, client suitability, concentration decisions and conflict management—not merely maintain policies.Review suitability files and conflict disclosures for higher-risk or illiquid instruments; confirm the investment rationale is recorded.
12 Jun 2026Federal Department of Finance · AMLA / LETAPrepare now

The revised Anti-Money Laundering Act, Legal Entities Transparency Act and implementing ordinances enter into force on 1 October 2026. A central transparency register will be introduced.

Open primary source ↗		AMLA-supervised financial intermediaries, including relevant Swiss asset managers, will be eligible to use the register for KYC and due-diligence purposes. Client onboarding and beneficial-owner workflows may need updating.Map affected entities, update beneficial-owner escalation procedures and plan authorised access to the register.
03 Jun 2026FINMA · Guidance 03/2026Control review

FINMA guidance focuses on risk identification across managed assets and confirms that responsibility remains with the institution when risk management or compliance is outsourced.

Open primary source ↗		Directly relevant where a portfolio manager relies on external compliance or risk providers: accountability, oversight and documented monitoring remain with the regulated firm.Check risk inventory coverage for liquidity, valuation, concentration and conflicts; document oversight of outsourced control functions.
06 May 2026FINMA · Asset-management data collectionRecurring obligation

FINMA published the 2026 asset-management survey and filing guidance. The collection supports FINMA's risk-oriented view of portfolio management, advisory and execution-only activity.

Open primary source ↗		The survey fields show the supervisory information FINMA expects firms to maintain consistently; filing evidence can be useful in a future supervisory discussion.Retain filing evidence and use the submitted data fields as an internal readiness checklist for the next reporting cycle.
