SIF · Planning item · Earliest 2029

FATCA Model 1 transition: prepare, but do not switch early

Switzerland’s move to reciprocal Model 1 remains planned, but SIF now states that entry into force is possible no earlier than 1 January 2029. Existing Model 2 obligations continue meanwhile.

What matters

  • Switzerland and the United States signed the Model 1 agreement on 27 June 2024.
  • The earliest stated effective date is 1 January 2029; legislation and operational rules must still be completed.
  • Transition provisions are intended to preserve continuity between Model 2 and Model 1.

Why it matters and what may need to be done

  • Continue the institution’s current Model 2 classification, GIIN/registration maintenance, client documentation, withholding and reporting process. Do not deregister or change responsible-officer arrangements merely because Model 1 has been signed.
  • Confirm the IAM’s current FATCA status and whether reporting is performed directly or by a bank, trustee, fund administrator or other provider; responsibility and evidence should be explicit in the contract and compliance calendar.
  • Keep self-certifications, account-opening forms, privacy notices and data-transfer wording current under the existing regime. Track expiry, curing of indicia and undocumented-account escalation.
  • Place the transition on the regulatory roadmap with a named owner and decision gates tied to enacted Swiss rules and official technical guidance—not to an assumed 2029 launch.
  • When rules are final, perform a gap analysis covering registration/status, reporting channel, data fields, consent/privacy, agreements with service providers, client remediation, testing and cutover reconciliation.
  • Contracts being renewed now may include change-in-law and cooperation clauses so providers must supply data and implement the future reporting model without reopening basic responsibility questions.
Practical evidence: FATCA classification memo; GIIN evidence; reporting calendar and receipts; provider responsibility matrix; exception log; annually reviewed transition roadmap.
