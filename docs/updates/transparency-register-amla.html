Federal Council · Effective 1 October 2026

Swiss transparency register: company filing, not automatic client-by-client checking

What changed: LETA, revised AMLA and the implementing ordinances enter into force on 1 October 2026. Most Swiss companies must report their beneficial owners to the federal register under the applicable transition rules. [S1] [S2]

Action plan

Applicability
UBO register Corporate reporting
Relevant to the IAM's own Swiss legal entity if it is within the reporting scope. [S1]
Accountable function
To be set internally
The public commencement notice does not allocate the IAM's internal role.
Due / trigger
Effective date: 1 October 2026. This starts statutory transition periods; it is not one universal filing deadline for every entity. The exact filing date must be determined from the entity's category and applicable provisions. [S1] [S2]
Status
Prepare own filing
  • Determine whether the IAM entity is required to report and identify the data required by the enacted framework. [S1]
  • Prepare AGOV/EasyGov access if the IAM will use that filing channel. [S4]
  • Calculate the entity-specific statutory filing date; do not substitute 1 October 2026 as the deadline.
Evidence
Submission through the official channel and the resulting filing confirmation, where reporting applies. [S3] [S4]

What matters

  • The Federal Office of Justice operates the non-public register, while an FDF control office checks completeness, accuracy and currency. [S1]
  • EasyGov supports lower-volume access for authorised users and preparation instructions are available. [S3] [S4]
  • LETA does not impose a systematic duty on financial intermediaries to query the register or compare it with client data. Existing AML due-diligence obligations remain unchanged. [S2, commentary to the discrepancy provisions]
  • If a financial intermediary identifies a discrepancy, the client is first given a reasonable opportunity to clarify it; any statutory reporting period then depends on the unresolved discrepancy mechanism. [S2]

Client due diligence

LETA does not create a systematic duty to query the register or compare it with client data. The immediate action item is therefore the IAM's own company reporting. A routine client-query workflow would be an internal control choice unless another applicable provision requires it in a particular case. [S2]
