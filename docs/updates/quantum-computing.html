FINMA Guidance 05/2026 · Medium relevance

Quantum risk becomes an operational-resilience planning issue

FINMA’s survey found broad awareness but too few clear migration roadmaps. The immediate task is inventory and planning, not an instant technology replacement.

FINMA’s measures

  • Create an institution-specific quantum risk analysis and a cryptographic inventory.
  • Protect critical data against “harvest now, decrypt later” attacks.
  • Develop a roadmap for quantum-safe encryption and crypto-agility.
  • Include external service providers in the assessment and transition.

Why it matters

  • A smaller manager may outsource nearly all technology, but it still depends on encryption in email, storage, client portals, backups and provider connections.
  • Long-lived client and KYC data may require protection before cryptographically relevant quantum computers exist.
  • Vendor roadmaps and contract cooperation will be central to a proportionate response.
Suggested follow-up: ask the IT provider for a cryptographic inventory and migration roadmap, identify long-retention sensitive data and add quantum readiness to the annual technology-risk review.
