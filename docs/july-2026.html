Ten selected developments for a Swiss independent asset manager. Updated 28 July 2026.

High relevance
FINMA · 03 Jun

FINMA sharpens expectations for products used in individual portfolio management

Recurring escalation cases show that product selection, liquidity and concentration risks, conflicts around proprietary products and oversight of outsourced controls must be demonstrable—not merely described in policies. Directly relevant to discretionary mandates.

High relevance
FINMA · 04 Jun

AML risk analysis must function as a real management control

FINMA found progress but continuing weaknesses in risk tolerance, exclusions and methodology at banks and FinIA institutions. Asset managers should be able to connect business model, quantitative limits and the allocation of AML resources.

Read the IAM analysis →
Prepare now
Federal Council · 12 Jun

Transparency register and revised AMLA start on 1 October 2026

Swiss legal entities enter a new beneficial-owner reporting regime, while AMLA-supervised firms gain controlled access to the register. The implementation date starts transition periods and calls for entity, KYC and discrepancy workflows to be mapped now.

High relevance
SAM / SECO · Jul

Sanctions searches need a replacement evidence trail

SECO’s online search no longer offers the former print/PDF function. A dated screenshot or equivalent tamper-evident record should be built into onboarding and periodic screening until a replacement is available.

Read the IAM analysis →
Plan, not act
SIF · Jul

Swiss–US FATCA Model 1 transition moves further out

The official SIF position now says entry into force is possible no earlier than 1 January 2029. Existing Model 2 processes remain in place; managers should track the future GIIN and self-certification transition but avoid premature operational changes.

High relevance
SAM · 08 Jul

Business introducers require a controlled onboarding framework

SAM and PwC’s checklist turns introducer relationships into a reviewable control set: role boundaries, remuneration, client communications, AML responsibilities, data protection, cross-border risk and termination should be explicit and documented.

Medium relevance
FINMA · 09 Jul

FINMA asks institutions to prepare for quantum-era cryptographic risk

FINMA expects forward planning: a cryptographic inventory, institution-specific risk analysis, vendor involvement and a roadmap toward quantum-safe encryption. Proportionality matters, but outsourced IT does not make the issue disappear.

Read the IAM analysis →
Medium relevance
COMCO · 23 Jul

Competition-law scrutiny reaches labour-market agreements

COMCO’s consultation focuses on potentially problematic wage-information exchanges and labour-market agreements. Asset managers should examine peer benchmarking, no-poach arrangements and HR association activity before the final note is issued.

Read the IAM analysis →
High relevance
EU / UK / US · Jul

Russia sanctions require a four-regime exposure review

The EU’s 21st package expands restrictions on banks, crypto platforms, energy, shadow-fleet vessels and military-industrial actors; the UK and US also changed Russia-related lists and guidance. Swiss applicability must be separated from EU, UK and US nexus requirements.

Read the IAM analysis →
High relevance
Federal Supreme Court · 17 Jun

Execution-only retrocessions: the Supreme Court confirms a conflict-based test

Retrocessions are not automatically repayable in a genuine execution-only relationship without a concrete conflict, including on the facts of in-house structured products. The result does not extend automatically to discretion, advice or product promotion.

Read the IAM analysis →

Watchlist — no immediate IAM action

  • FINMA liquidity ordinance: effective 1 January 2027 for banks and securities firms; not directly applicable to a conventional independent portfolio manager, but relevant to regulated counterparties.
  • Swiss implementation of the EU 21st sanctions package: monitor SECO and Federal Council decisions; do not represent EU measures as already incorporated into Swiss law before publication and entry into force.
  • Federal Supreme Court / Gazprombank: the April judgments reinforce substantive verification of beneficial ownership where PEP, offshore and other red flags remain unresolved. This is reflected in the AML workstream above.
