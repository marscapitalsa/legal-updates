Action required

These developments require an implementation step or review. Where no external deadline is set, the internal deadline remains to be determined.

Action required
FINMA · 04 Jun

AML risk analysis must connect tolerance, limits and control effectiveness

What changed: FINMA explained how the institution-wide analysis should connect the business model, approved risk tolerance, meaningful quantitative indicators, exceptions and net risk.

Deadline: to be set internally · FINMA states no new calendar deadline

Action: check that the IAM's analysis covers all material criteria, uses client-count and AuM measures, governs exceptions and supports its control-effectiveness conclusions.

Action required
Federal Council · 12 Jun

Transparency register starts on 1 October 2026

What changed: the new federal beneficial-owner register and revised AMLA framework enter into force on 1 October 2026, starting the applicable transition periods.

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · exact entity filing date depends on the statutory category

Action: determine the IAM entity's reporting scope and beneficial-owner data, prepare the official filing channel and calculate the entity-specific deadline. The law does not impose systematic client-register checks.

Action if used
SAM / SECO · Jul

SECO search results can no longer be printed or saved as before

What changed: the former print/save function is unavailable; SAM recommends a date- and time-stamped screenshot while SECO considers replacement functionality.

Deadline: to be set internally · no external deadline stated

Action: if the IAM's manual-search procedure relied on the removed function, replace that evidence step with the screenshot method described by SAM.

Action if used
SAM / PwC · Jul

SAM publishes a contract checklist for business introducers

What changed: SAM circulated a non-exhaustive PwC checklist for reviewing an existing introducer agreement or preparing a new one.

Deadline: to be set internally · no external deadline stated

Action: if introducers are used, compare the agreement with the checklist, keep the role to pure introductions unless separately analysed, retain AML/KYC onboarding with the IAM and check cross-border authority.

Action if used
FINMA · 03 Jun

FINMA details controls for higher-risk products in individual mandates

What changed: FINMA addressed suitability, objective product selection, conflicts, risk-based due diligence and risk control after cases involving foreign funds, AMCs, own and illiquid products.

Deadline: to be set internally · no new calendar deadline stated

Action: where affected products are selected or held, check suitability at portfolio level, objective selection and conflicts, the warning signs identified by FINMA, and whether risk/compliance covers concentration, liquidity and valuation.

Action required
FINMA · 09 Jul

FINMA recommends a post-quantum roadmap by mid-2027

What changed: FINMA set out expectations for quantum risk analysis, cryptographic inventories, migration planning, crypto-agility and provider dependencies.

FINMA recommended target: mid-2027 · not a statutory commencement date

Action: assess applicability, inventory cryptographic dependencies and prepare an implementation plan and post-quantum roadmap covering outsourced services.

Potentially relevant

No immediate action is required unless the stated condition applies. The condition for relevance is shown first.

Potentially relevant
EU / UK / US · Jul

New foreign Russia-sanctions measures

What changed: the EU adopted its 21st package, the UK announced cyber designations and OFAC updated targeted Russia-related licences.

Relevant if: the IAM or an affected client, transaction, custodian, bank, security or provider has an EU, UK or US nexus. These foreign measures do not themselves amend Swiss law or require every Swiss IAM to rescreen.

Potentially relevant
SIF · 29 Jun

FATCA Model 1 cannot start before 1 January 2029

What changed: SIF moved the earliest possible commencement of reciprocal Model 1 to 1 January 2029; implementation work continues.

Relevant if: the IAM is within the FATCA framework. Continue under Model 2 and monitor official implementation; the update states no new operational task.

Potentially relevant
COMCO · 21 Jul

Draft guidance addresses competition in labour markets

What changed: COMCO opened consultation on wage coordination, no-poach or no-hire arrangements and exchanges of employment information.

Relevant if: the IAM participates in those arrangements or exchanges. The 30 September 2026 date applies only to an optional consultation response.

Potentially relevant
Federal Supreme Court · 17 Jun

Execution-only retrocessions depend on the actual relationship and conflict

What changed: the Court found no repayment duty in genuine execution-only cases involving client-selected dual-currency deposits because no relevant conflict existed on the facts.

Relevant if: the IAM actually provides execution-only services and receives product-related remuneration. The outcome does not extend automatically to discretionary management, advice or every in-house product.
