FINMA Guidance 03/2026 · High relevance

Products in individual portfolio management: evidence is the control

FINMA’s escalation cases make product governance, client-interest safeguards and risk ownership a direct supervisory priority for portfolio managers under Article 17 FinIA.

What the source says

  • Cases involved foreign collective investments, structured products including AMCs, and securities issued through issuing or structuring companies.
  • FINMA stresses early risk identification, robust governance and consistent client-focused FinSA conduct.
  • Outsourcing compliance or risk management does not transfer the institution’s responsibility.

Why it matters

  • Product approval should evidence liquidity, valuation, concentration, issuer and conflict risks.
  • Where own and third-party products compete, the conflict and client-protection measures should be recorded.
  • Mandate suitability and portfolio rationale need a traceable link to the client profile and investment strategy.
Suggested follow-up: sample higher-risk positions and confirm that product approval, selection rationale, mandate fit, conflicts and ongoing monitoring are all evidenced. Test oversight of outsourced control providers.
