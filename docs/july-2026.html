Action required

These developments require a documented implementation step, control change or exposure review. Open the topic page for the detailed action plan.

Action required
FINMA · 04 Jun

AML risk analysis must function as a real management control

Deadline: 31 August 2026 · thereafter annually and upon material change

Required now: review the AML risk analysis and internal AML documentation; translate risk appetite into measurable limits, escalation triggers and resource allocation, and obtain documented approval.

Action required
Federal Council · 12 Jun

Transparency register and revised AMLA start on 1 October 2026

Deadline: 30 September 2026 · operational readiness before commencement

Required now: identify the entity's beneficial owners, prepare the reporting data and assign responsibility for filings, KYC use of the register and discrepancy handling before the new regime starts.

Action required
SAM / SECO · Jul

Sanctions searches need a replacement evidence trail

Deadline: 7 August 2026 · evidence required for every screening event immediately

Required now: amend onboarding and periodic-screening procedures so every SECO search produces dated, retrievable evidence, such as a screenshot or equivalent tamper-evident record.

Action required
SAM · 08 Jul

Business introducers require a controlled onboarding framework

Deadline: 31 August 2026 · new introducers must be cleared before appointment

Required now: inventory active introducers and document role boundaries, remuneration, client communications, AML allocation, data protection, cross-border restrictions, monitoring and termination controls.

Priority 2

Potentially relevant

No immediate implementation is required unless the stated condition applies. The condition is shown first so readers can decide whether to open the analysis.

Potentially relevant
FINMA · 03 Jun

FINMA sharpens expectations for products used in individual portfolio management

Relevant if: the IAM launches, issues, selects or manages an AMC or another complex, illiquid, concentrated or proprietary product. In that case product approval, enhanced due diligence, liquidity testing, conflicts controls and ongoing monitoring must be designed before use.

Potentially relevant
SIF · Jul

Swiss–US FATCA Model 1 transition moves further out

Relevant if: the IAM maintains FATCA procedures or supports an entity affected by the future Model 1 transition. Continue current Model 2 processes; no operational change is required before implementation details are fixed.

Read the conditional analysis →
Potentially relevant
FINMA · 09 Jul

FINMA asks institutions to prepare for quantum-era cryptographic risk

Relevant if: the IAM stores long-lived confidential data, operates material cryptographic systems or relies on IT providers whose migration planning must be challenged. This is a roadmap and vendor-governance issue, not an immediate technology replacement.

Potentially relevant
COMCO · 23 Jul

Competition-law scrutiny reaches labour-market agreements

Relevant if: the IAM exchanges non-public salary information, participates in compensation benchmarking or uses no-poach or non-solicitation arrangements with other employers.

Read the conditional analysis →
Potentially relevant
Federal Supreme Court · 17 Jun

Execution-only retrocessions: the Supreme Court confirms a conflict-based test

Relevant if: the IAM provides genuine execution-only services, receives retrocessions or uses in-house structured products. The judgment should not be extrapolated to discretionary management, advice or product promotion.

Watchlist — no immediate IAM action

  • FINMA liquidity ordinance: effective 1 January 2027 for banks and securities firms; not directly applicable to a conventional independent portfolio manager, but relevant to regulated counterparties.
  • Swiss implementation of the EU 21st sanctions package: monitor SECO and Federal Council decisions; do not represent EU measures as already incorporated into Swiss law before publication and entry into force.
  • Federal Supreme Court / Gazprombank: the April judgments reinforce substantive verification of beneficial ownership where PEP, offshore and other red flags remain unresolved. This is reflected in the AML workstream above.
