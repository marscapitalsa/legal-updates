SIF · Official implementation status

FATCA Model 1 cannot enter into force before 1 January 2029

What changed: SIF's 29 June 2026 update says the reciprocal Model 1 agreement, signed on 27 June 2024, can enter into force no earlier than 1 January 2029. The implementing legislation is still under preparation. [S1]

What the source confirms

  • Switzerland currently applies FATCA Model 2. [S1]
  • The Model 1 agreement is reciprocal and was signed on 27 June 2024. [S1]
  • Implementation work is under way; the earliest possible commencement is 1 January 2029 and transition provisions are intended to ensure continuity. [S1]

What was removed from the earlier version

The SIF update does not order a new classification memorandum, GIIN review, provider matrix, form update, contract clause, testing plan or transition roadmap. Those may be useful internal FATCA controls, but they do not follow from this publication and are therefore not presented as actions. SAM's July estimate of 2028 is not used because the later official SIF update states 2029.
