SIF · Planning item

FATCA Model 1 transition is possible no earlier than 2029

The direction remains automatic and reciprocal exchange, but the official timetable has moved. Existing Model 2 compliance continues until the new agreement becomes effective.

Key facts

  • Switzerland and the United States signed the Model 1 agreement on 27 June 2024.
  • SIF’s current page states that the earliest entry into force is 1 January 2029.
  • Special transition provisions are intended to preserve continuity between Models 2 and 1.

Why it matters

  • Registered deemed-compliant FFIs should not deregister or change GIIN processes prematurely.
  • Future changes may affect registration status, self-certifications, responsible officers and service-provider workflows.
  • Contracts and client documentation should be capable of accommodating the later transition.
Suggested follow-up: keep the item on the regulatory roadmap with a named owner and annual review; continue current Model 2 filings and wait for operational rules before changing registrations.
