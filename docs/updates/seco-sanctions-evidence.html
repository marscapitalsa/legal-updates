SAM clarification with SECO · High relevance

Sanctions screening needs a new evidence trail

The removal of SECO’s former print/PDF function is operationally small but control-significant: a search that cannot later be evidenced may be difficult to defend.

What changed

  • According to SAM’s July Newsflash, SECO confirmed that direct printing or saving of search results is no longer available in its previous form.
  • SECO is assessing whether similar functionality can be restored.
  • In the interim, SECO suggested dated and time-stamped screenshots; its search engine remains a supporting tool rather than a complete due-diligence solution.

Why it matters

  • Onboarding, payment and periodic-review files should show the query, result, date, time and reviewer.
  • A negative result should not displace spelling variants, ownership/control analysis or other sanctions sources.
  • Locally saved screenshots need retention and access controls consistent with the client file.
Suggested follow-up: amend the sanctions-screening procedure and checklist, define an approved capture method and filename convention, and test that evidence is retained in the relevant client record.
