FINMA Guidance 05/2026 · Supervisory recommendation

Quantum risk: FINMA recommends a post-quantum roadmap by mid-2027

What changed: FINMA published expectations for institutions exposed to quantum-computing risks and recommends that their strategic planning include a post-quantum cryptography roadmap by mid-2027. [S1] [S2]

Action plan

Applicability
Operational risk Outsourcing
Relevant if cryptography protects the IAM's data, processes or systems, including outsourced services. [S2]
Responsible person / function
Proposed internal allocation:
Primary: in-house Risk Manager as operational-risk owner
Technical inventory and migration input: ICT service providers and the internal outsourcing owner. Contract and data-protection review: in-house Legal Counsel. Roadmap approval, resources and risk acceptance: Directors. [S2] [S3] [S4]
Due / trigger
FINMA recommended target: mid-2027 for a post-quantum cryptography roadmap. This is a supervisory recommendation, not a statutory entry-into-force date. [S1] [S2]
Status
Planning required
  • Perform an institution-specific quantum risk analysis. [S2]
  • Create and continuously update a cryptographic inventory. [S2]
  • Prepare the roadmap, including vulnerable algorithms and migration priorities. [S2]
  • Cover external providers and crypto-agility in procurement and development. [S2]
Evidence
Risk analysis, cryptographic inventory, implementation plan with milestones and target dates, and post-quantum roadmap. [S2]

What matters

  • The risk analysis should cover business processes, ICT systems, applications, infrastructure, outsourced services and service providers. [S2]
  • The inventory should identify cryptography for data in transit and at rest, signatures, key management and authentication. [S2]
  • Long-lived confidential data creates “harvest now, decrypt later” exposure and affects prioritisation. [S2]
  • FINMA expects implementation planning with milestones, priorities and target dates and recommends hybrid solutions where appropriate to the risk. [S2]

Implementation boundary

The Guidance does not require immediate replacement of all encryption, name a particular IAM employee, mandate a vendor questionnaire format or prescribe individual test intervals. Those details must be set internally within the risk analysis and roadmap.
