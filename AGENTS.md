# IAM Regulatory Brief — editorial rules

- Base every factual statement, obligation, recommended action, deadline and quantitative threshold on a specific publication, decision or document.
- Link the exact source, not a generic regulator, association or law-firm landing page. Add page or section references where available.
- Distinguish legal obligations, supervisory expectations, court holdings, association recommendations and law-firm analysis. Do not present one category as another.
- Do not invent deadlines, owners, controls, evidence requirements or numerical limits. If no external deadline exists, use `To be set internally`.
- Label any necessary inference or company-specific application as such and identify the facts on which it depends.
- If sources conflict, use the more authoritative and current source and disclose the practical difference where it matters.
- Keep the reader-facing language neutral and concise: use `Action plan`, `Evidence` and `Sources`. Do not add labels such as `source-based`, `source-supported` or `source-verified`.
- Keep citations and direct source links in each article, but do not publish a separate methodology page or editorial disclaimer about the verification process.
- Do not maintain a separate action register. Put the complete action plan on each topic page, especially for priority and action-required items.
- Every action plan must include `Responsible person / function`, separating primary execution, control or review, and director approval or oversight where relevant.
- For the current IAM structure, consider Directors, the designated AML Officer, the outsourced compliance/AML provider, in-house Legal Counsel and the in-house Risk Manager. Add Portfolio Management, ICT providers, tax advisers or other functions only where the topic requires them.
- Do not assign operational responsibility to a shareholder solely because of share ownership. A shareholder is responsible only where that person also acts in a relevant formal capacity or has a specific legal information or reporting duty.
