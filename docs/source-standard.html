Method

Source and inference standard

The brief separates source facts, legal or supervisory status, conditional relevance and internal implementation decisions.

July 2026Action Register

Publication rule

  • Every material factual statement, obligation, recommendation and external deadline must be traceable to a specific publication, decision or document. A regulator's or association's generic landing page is not sufficient.
  • Primary law, regulator guidance, court decisions, association recommendations and law-firm analysis are labelled separately. A recommendation is not presented as a legal obligation.
  • Page, section or item references are included where the source permits them.
  • No action, owner, deadline, quantitative threshold or closure evidence is attributed to a source unless the source supports it.
  • Where the source leaves implementation to the institution, the brief says to be set internally. Internal decisions must not be described as regulatory deadlines.
  • An inference may be included only when it is expressly labelled as an inference or conditional application and the facts supporting it are identified.
  • If sources conflict, the more authoritative and current source controls; the discrepancy is disclosed.

Action-plan labels

  • Source-based action: the action is expressly required or recommended by the cited document.
  • Conditional review: relevance depends on a legal nexus, product, service or factual condition that the publication does not determine for the IAM.
  • To be set internally: the source specifies no external owner or deadline; the institution must decide it separately.
  • No action stated: the publication is informational or preparatory and does not yet require implementation.
Correction rule: if a statement cannot be tied to a specific source, it is removed or rewritten as a clearly identified internal proposal. Topic pages retain exact source links so a reader can verify the conclusion directly.
