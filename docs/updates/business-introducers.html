Business introducers: contract boundaries and practical checks

What changed: SAM circulated a PwC checklist for reviewing an existing business-introducer agreement or preparing a new one. It is a practical, non-exhaustive checklist—not a FINMA rule and not a guarantee of FinIA, FinSA or foreign-law compliance. [S1] [S2, p. 1]

What the checklist actually says

  • Permitted service: the contemplated service is a pure introduction. The checklist proposes clauses preventing the introducer from representing the IAM, recommending or advising on its behalf, concluding contracts, accepting offers, using the IAM's brand, marketing or distributing financial instruments, or carrying out the IAM's AML checks. [S2, pp. 1–2]
  • Additional services change the analysis: identifying the contracting party or beneficial owner, or preparing KYC documentation, may amount to delegation of an essential task and can require inclusion in organisational regulations and prior FINMA approval. Other services can raise employee, foreign-branch or permanent-establishment issues. [S2, p. 2, footnote 1]
  • Independent status: for a natural-person introducer, the checklist recommends verifying independent status annually. It also proposes representations covering authority, required registrations or licences, AML and anti-corruption compliance, and an annual certificate that the representations remain correct. [S2, pp. 3, 5]
  • Cross-border responsibility: the IAM remains responsible for checking and monitoring whether the introducer may act in the jurisdiction concerned. The contract or directives should identify prohibited acts. [S2, pp. 3–5]
  • IAM onboarding: the IAM must retain its own onboarding process without exception. Information from the introducer may be useful, but the IAM remains responsible for KYC. [S2, p. 6]
  • Remuneration paid by the IAM: the agreement should define the type and calculation of remuneration and payment information. The IAM should ensure that remuneration is compatible with its remuneration system and applicable law. The checklist also addresses the introducer's information to prospects and the IAM's disclosure of remuneration on request. [S2, p. 7]
  • Contract clauses: the remaining checklist sections address confidentiality and data protection, conflicts, records and return or destruction of information, liability, duration and termination. [S2, pp. 8–10]
